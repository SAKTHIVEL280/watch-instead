# Watch Instead privacy policy

**Effective date:** 5 November 2026  
**Contact:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/SAKTHIVEL280/watch-instead/issues)

Watch Instead has no accounts, ads or analytics, and we run no servers.

## Data on your device
Your saved links, notes and settings stay on your device. They are not included in cloud backups.
Uninstalling Watch Instead deletes them.
If you export a backup, you choose where the file is saved. It is not encrypted, so keep it
somewhere you trust.

## Accessibility service
Watch Instead uses Android's AccessibilityService API to detect when an app on your guarded list comes to
the foreground, so it can show one of your saved videos. If you set the cooldown to None or add a focus schedule, it also detects when you
return to your home screen, so it knows when to prompt again. It does not read screen content, typed
text, messages or passwords. This information is used only on your device and is never collected
or shared. The service is optional and can be turned off at any time in Android settings.

## Stats
Watch Instead keeps a history of its prompts on your device: which guarded app, when, what you chose and
any reason you tapped. It powers your stats, is never collected or shared, can be cleared in
Settings, and is included in backups you export.

## Network requests
When you save a link, Watch Instead requests its title and thumbnail. For YouTube and TikTok links the
request goes to that service's public embed endpoint, and for other links it goes to the linked
website. Thumbnails are then loaded from the address that service provides. These services receive
the link and your IP address, as with any web request.

## Opening videos
Opening a saved video hands it to the matching app or your browser, whose own privacy terms apply.

## Children
Watch Instead is not directed at children under 13.
